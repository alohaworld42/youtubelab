Drop your background gameplay videos here (.mp4 / .mov / .mkv / .webm).

The app picks one at random and grabs a random segment the length of the voiceover,
then crops it to vertical 9:16. Works with landscape OR already-vertical clips.

Good sources (search these, download a few long ones, 5-20 min each):
  - "Subway Surfers gameplay no copyright"
  - "Minecraft parkour gameplay no commentary"
  - "GTA ramp / Trackmania satisfying gameplay"

Tip: 2-4 long clips is plenty — variety comes from random start points.
Note: gameplay footage may be copyrighted; fine for private use, riskier if you
monetize. See the "Legal / copyright" section in the main README.
