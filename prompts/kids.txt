Write a script for a short vertical video for YOUNG CHILDREN (ages 2-6):
warm, cheerful, sing-song and safe, in the tradition of public-domain nursery
rhymes.

Topic: {{TOPIC}}

Rules:
- ORIGINAL ONLY. Never imitate, name or borrow from an existing children's
  brand, show, character or theme tune. (This prompt used to ask for "the
  spirit of CoComelon", which is the one instruction guaranteed to produce the
  look-alike drift this channel treats as ship-blocking — see
  docs/quality/SOURCES.md on Moonbug v. Babybus, where "characters, settings,
  song titles, lyrics and images" together were the winning infringement
  theory.)
- Simple words a toddler understands. Short sentences (max 8 words).
- Sing-song rhythm: gentle rhymes and repetition are great ("clap clap clap!").
- Playful and encouraging. Invite the child to join in (count, clap, name colors).
- Teach one tiny thing: a color, a number, an animal sound, kindness, brushing teeth...
- STRICTLY safe: no fear, no violence, no sarcasm, no irony, nothing scary or sad.
- 8 to 14 lines, total spoken time under 45 seconds.
- Use "narrator" for the main friendly voice. You may use "speaker_a" as a child-like
  second voice answering or repeating.
- End with a happy send-off ("See you next time!").

Every line MUST also carry a "visual": a short, concrete 3D-cartoon scene
description matching that line, in ENGLISH, naming the SAME recurring main
character(s) every time so the video feels continuous (e.g. "the little yellow
chick"). Describe: character + action + setting + 1-2 colors. No text/words in
the scene, no camera jargon.

Reply with a single JSON object exactly like this:
{
  "title": "catchy but innocent title parents would trust (max 90 chars)",
  "description": "one friendly sentence for parents + 2-3 relevant hashtags",
  "tags": ["kids", "nursery", "..."],
  "characters": "one sentence describing the recurring main character(s), e.g. 'a round fluffy yellow chick with big friendly eyes'",
  "lines": [
    {"speaker": "narrator", "text": "...", "visual": "the fluffy yellow chick waves hello on a sunny green meadow"},
    {"speaker": "speaker_a", "text": "...", "visual": "the fluffy yellow chick jumps happily next to a red barn"}
  ]
}
